\# LETTERS TO THE FUTURE



Format for the first book. 12 letters.



\## Letter Structure:

1\. Address (Friend, You who reads...)

2\. One main thought

3\. Story / metaphor / image

4\. Practice or question

5\. Signature (with symbols)



\## Themes of 12 Letters:

1\. Who writes (introduction)

2\. Desire as beginning of everything

3\. Thought as form of desire

4\. Action as bridge

5\. Result as mirror

6\. Fear and its nature

7\. Time (it doesn't exist, but it does)

8\. Connection (between all of us)

9\. Creativity as way of being

10\. Mistake as teacher

11\. Light inside

12\. Until we meet (not goodbye)



\## Tone:

\- Warm, brotherly

\- Without preaching

\- From experience, not theory

\- With humor where appropriate



\## Length:

\- 2-4 pages per letter

\- ~500-1000 words
