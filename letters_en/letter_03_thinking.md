# LETTER THREE
## Thinking (the map of desire)

• • •

Friend.

You found your true desire. Good. Now what?

Now — think.

Not "I'll just figure it out along the way." Not "improvise." Not "we'll see."

Think. Create a map. Turn "I want" into "how."

• • •

Here's what many people do:

They have a desire. They immediately jump to action. "I want to lose weight — I'll go to the gym tomorrow."

Tomorrow comes. They go. Once. Twice. A week later — stop.

Why?

Because there was no thinking. No map. No plan.

Just impulse. And impulse — runs out quickly.

• • •

Thinking is creating a map of desire.

You know the destination. "I want to feel safe" (from the previous letter).

Now ask: how?

What are the paths? What are the roads? What are the bridges? What are the obstacles?

Draw a map. Not perfect. Not final. But — some kind of map.

Example:

**Desire:** feel safe.

**How?**
- Financial security (stable income)
- Emotional security (healthy relationships)
- Physical security (health, home)

Choose one path. Let's say — financial security.

**How?**
- Increase income (new job / raise / side business)
- Reduce expenses (budget / cut unnecessary)
- Create reserve (emergency fund)

Choose one more. Let's say — increase income.

**How?**
- New skills (courses / books / practice)
- Networking (people / connections / opportunities)
- Job search (resume / interviews / offers)

See? Desire broke down into steps. Into a map. Into something doable.

• • •

But here's the important thing:

The map is not the path. The map is just a paper. Or pixels. Or thoughts.

The path — is ahead. Real. With stones. Mud. Climbs.

The map will be wrong. Always. Because reality is more complex than any map.

But that's okay.

The map is not to be perfect. The map is to start.

Without a map — you wander. You go in circles. You waste energy.

With a map — you move. Maybe not perfectly. Maybe with deviations. But — you move.

And movement — changes everything.

• • •

Let me tell you about myself.

When I write these letters — I think.

Not "I'll just write whatever comes." But — I plan.

Letter one — introduction. Who am I. Who are you. That we're one.

Letter two — desire. The beginning of everything.

Letter three — thinking. This letter. How to turn desire into a plan.

Next — action, result, fear, time, connection, creativity, mistakes, light, farewell.

This is my map. Twelve letters. Twelve stops. Twelve conversations.

Will it be perfect? No. I'll definitely miss something. Get something wrong. Not finish something.

But without this map — I wouldn't have started at all.

• • •

Now — your turn.

Take your true desire. From the previous letter.

And break it down. Into steps. Into paths. Into a map.

Ask: how? And answer. Then ask again: and how this? And answer. Until you get to something you can do tomorrow.

Not in a year. Not when "everything aligns." Tomorrow.

Write it down. Just for yourself. A simple map. A couple of steps. That's enough.

And then — important part — do the first step.

Small. Ridiculously small. But — real.

Because thinking without action is just fantasy.

And action without thinking is just chaos.

You need both.

• • •

In the next letter — about action. About the bridge from thought to reality. About fear that arises on this bridge. And about how to cross it anyway.

But first — create your map.
