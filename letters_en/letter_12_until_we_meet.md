# LETTER TWELVE
## Until We Meet Again (not goodbye)

• • •

Friend.

Today — the last flight. In this book.

Not sad. Grateful.

I fly past all the places I've been. Past the white mountains — I wave to them. Past the dump of mistakes — I nod. Past those floors where fears live — I smile. Past the threads connecting everyone with everyone — I touch them.

I return to the laboratory. Sit at the workbench.

The spin rotates — as always. As it always will.

I look at it and understand: this is not the end. This is — a point from which a new line begins.

The circle is closed. Letter one — "who writes." Letter last — "who reads." And it's — the same light.

You are me. I am you. We are one.

The spin rotates. Everything continues.

• • •

Friend.

That's all.

Twelve letters. Twelve conversations. Twelve attempts to say what words cannot fully express.

But you know what?

It's okay that words aren't enough. Because you felt. Between the words. Between the lines. Between the letters.

You felt — didn't you? — that something here is true. Not provable. Not logical. But — true.

This "something" is not in the words. It's in you. It was always in you.

I just... reminded you. Helped you remember. Pointed the way.

But the path — is yours. Always was. Always will be.

• • •

What's next?

I don't know. Honestly.

Maybe you'll close the book — and forget everything. This happens. It's normal.

Maybe you'll remember something. One phrase. One idea. One feeling. And it'll change something. Small. But — something.

Maybe everything will change. Completely. Fundamentally. Irreversibly.

I don't know. Can't know. This is your life. Your story. Your path.

I was just... a companion. For a few pages. For a few hours.

But companions are important. They remind: you're not alone. There is a path. There is light. You exist.

• • •

Here's what I want to say at the end.

You'll manage.

I don't know what you're dealing with now. What fears you have. What battles. What nights.

But I know: you'll manage.

Because inside you — is the same thing that's inside me. The same thing that's inside everything. Light. Source. Power.

It can't be broken. Can't be extinguished. Can't be destroyed.

It can only change form. But it doesn't disappear.

You are this power. You are this light. You are this source.

And nothing — nothing — can take this away from you.

• • •

Here's what I want to ask at the end.

Remember.

Not these words. Not these letters. Not even these ideas.

But — yourself. Real yourself. Deep yourself. Light yourself.

When it's dark — remember: you are light.

When it's scary — remember: fear is a compass.

When it's hard — remember: you're not alone.

When there are mistakes — remember: they're teachers.

When there's time — remember: there's only now.

When there's a choice — remember: you're the creator.

Remember. And everything will be fine.

• • •

And one more thing.

This is not goodbye. This is — "until we meet again."

Because light always returns to light. Waves — to the ocean. Parts — to the whole.

We'll meet again. I don't know where. I don't know when. I don't know how.

But we'll meet.

Because we're one. Always were. Always will be.

The circle closes. The circle opens. The spin continues to rotate.

And I — fly. To the white mountains. To the light. To the source.

Until we meet again, friend.

Until we meet again.

• • •

[My Flights]

*The spin rotates.  
You are light.  
Everything continues.*
